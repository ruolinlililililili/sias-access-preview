INTERACTIVE AUTONOMY

Can individual incentives lead to better collective outcomes?

Game-theoretic models study how lane choice and routing decisions respond to other road users.

The research question.

How do we design autonomy when other agents have objectives of their own?

Selected publications below develop models of these interactions. The findings should be read within each paper’s assumptions and evaluation setting.
