A $2M NSF FIRE award launches our next chapter in wildfire evacuation research

How can people, AI, and autonomous systems coordinate when every minute matters? With a four-year, $2M NSF FIRE award, Ruolin Li and co-PIs Costas Synolakis, Ketan Savla, and Yue Zhao are taking on that question through EVAC-AI. The project brings together agentic AI and multi-agent decision support for wildfire evacuation. We’re excited to begin this work together—and grateful for the support that makes it possible.

New Funding

New NCST funding: fleet competition and the future of mixed-autonomy networks

How will competing companies operate fleets of autonomous and human-driven vehicles on shared roads—and what will that mean for transportation policy? NCST is supporting our project, “Strategic Interactions in Mixed-Autonomy Networks: Multi-Company AV/HV Fleet Operations and Policy Implications,” to explore these questions. We’re grateful to the National Center for Sustainable Transportation for supporting this work on the connections between fleet decisions, network outcomes, and policy.

Student Achievements

Fleet strategies, shared roads: our poster at ICCOPT 2025

How do fleet companies’ strategic choices shape the transportation networks we all share? At ICCOPT 2025, we presented our poster on traffic equilibrium in mixed-autonomy networks, connecting fleet operations with customer waiting and travel choices. This joint work by Jiaxin Hou, Kexin Wang, Ruolin Li, and Jong-Shi Pang brings company decisions and travelers’ experiences into the same picture. A happy moment to share the work—and be together at the poster!
